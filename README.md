# Business Policy & Strategic Management: Mid-sem Study Guide

A study site for the BPSM mid-semester exam (Units 1 and 2), built from the class notes first and the prescribed textbooks second.

**Live site:** https://swas204.github.io/bpsm-study-guide/

## Pages

| Page | Covers |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Start here: syllabus checklist, how the units fit together, exam tips |
| `unit1.html` | 1.1 to 1.7: business policy, evolution of SM, strategy concepts, levels of strategy, strategic intent, fit and flexibility, sustainability and governance |
| `unit2a.html` | 2.1 to 2.5: environmental scanning, PESTEL, Five Forces, industry life cycle, competitor analysis and benchmarking |
| `unit2b.html` | 2.6 to 2.11: internal environment, RBV, value chain, core competencies, VRIO, SWOT and gap analysis |
| `qbank-unit1.html` | Unit 1 question bank (5, 10 and 15-mark questions with model answers) |
| `qbank-unit2.html` | Unit 2 question bank |

## Reading tools

Mark sections as done, filter to undone only, hide the sidebar, and switch between dark and light themes. Progress is saved in your browser.

## Sources

Class notes, then Wheelen, Hunger, Hoffman & Bamford, *Strategic Management and Business Policy*; Grant, *Contemporary Strategy Analysis*; and Hill, Schilling & Jones, *Strategic Management: Theory and Cases*. Each section names its source.
