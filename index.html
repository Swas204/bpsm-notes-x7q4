How Units 1 and 2 fit together

The two units follow the order a firm actually thinks in. Unit 1 asks what strategy is and where the firm wants to go. Unit 2 asks what the firm is up against outside and what it has inside. SWOT, the last heading, joins the two.

Topics the class notes skip

None of the three notes copies has anything on the industry life cycle, competitor analysis and benchmarking or the value chain, and PESTEL and Five Forces appear only as lists. Those sections are written from the books. If the professor covers them in a later class, his version wins for the exam.

Answering in the exam

The professor's own tip, from the notes

Define, then quote the person who coined it. Open every answer with a one-line definition and the name behind it: Schendel and Hatten for strategy, Prahalad and Hamel for strategic intent and core competencies, Barney for RBV and VRIO, Porter for Five Forces and the value chain, Albert Humphrey for SWOT.

Assumed paper pattern

This follows the usual CHRIST mid-sem layout: Section A has 5-mark applied questions, Section B has 10-mark questions, and Section C is a 15-mark case split into two parts. Marking is by points. Bold point headings tied to the company in the question score better than general theory, and every 10 and 15-mark answer needs a short conclusion. Draw the diagram wherever a framework has one.
